Game product development workflow

How I take a game from market idea to live product.

Select a step to see how I applied it on Cara Cruz.

1

Market research & opportunity

Market trends, competitor games, player preferences and gaps worth filling.

Cara Cruz
  • Watched how cara y cruz is actually played, using gameplay videos from different platforms.
  • Interviewed people who play cara y cruz to understand the real experience.
  • Studied existing BingoPlus studio games to base the betting flow on.

WhySo the game feels authentic to the street game, and still familiar to current BingoPlus players.

2

Concept & game design

Concept, target experience, mechanics, rules and the core gameplay loop.

Cara Cruz
  • Kept the core loop simple: pick a side, watch the coin flip, see the result.
  • Used real coins instead of colourful studio coins, matching how the game is really played.
  • Set the game on a Filipino street to complete the story and packaging.
  • Showed bets as paper bills instead of coins, like players bet in the real game.

WhyAuthenticity is the hook: the closer it feels to the real street game, the more natural it is for Filipino players.

3

Requirements & specs

Feature requirements, technical documentation, system logic and user flows.

Cara Cruz
  • Worked side by side with the Product Manager to define how the game is presented on screen.
4

Feasibility & rapid prototyping

Prove the mechanics and features can be built well, before full production.

Cara Cruz
  • Built several prototypes of the coin-flip feel.
  • Prototyped the camera movement and camera position.
  • Prototyped different backgrounds for the game scene.

WhyTest what feels right early, before committing to full production.

5

Testing & evaluation

Check gameplay, usability, functionality and whether it meets the brief.

Cara Cruz
  • Designed a controlled coin-flip physics system instead of fully random physics.
  • Tuned it until it balanced realism with a satisfying game feel.
  • Experimented with different betting mechanics, the speed of each round and the overall betting flow.
  • Checked that every part, from placing a bet to the flip and the result, felt like one cohesive experience that keeps players engaged.

WhyPure physics can feel random or anticlimactic, and a slow or confusing betting flow breaks the rhythm. Tuning both keeps each round readable, exciting and easy to play again.

6

Cross-functional production

Coordinate developers, artists, backend engineers and product stakeholders.

Cara Cruz
  • Led a team of 6 across PM, Frontend, Backend, Design and Art.
  • Coordinated with the Marketing team.
  • Built for the BingoPlus app with Unity WebGL.
  • Acted as the link between the different teams, making sure information stayed consistent across every team and the project stayed on track.

WhyWith many teams working in parallel, one person keeping everyone aligned prevents mixed messages and delays.

7

Launch & optimization

Player feedback, analytics and performance drive the next version.

Cara Cruz
  • Released in the BingoPlus app.
↻Steps 4–5 loop until the prototype feels right for the market,↻and step 7 feeds back into step 1 for each new version.
